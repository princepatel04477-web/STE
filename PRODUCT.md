# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are exhibitors looking to book stalls for a premium B2B textile exhibition. Secondary users are verified B2B buyers and trade visitors seeking direct manufacturer sourcing, wholesale access, and networking with suppliers.

## Product Purpose

Position STE as India’s premium B2B textile sourcing platform and convert qualified exhibitors and buyers through clear trust signals, practical business utility, and direct booking pathways. Success is high-volume stall booking inquiries, verified buyer registrations, and frictionless WhatsApp or callback conversions.

## Brand Personality

Luxury, insightful, growth-oriented.

## Anti-references

Avoid vague cinematic-only fashion showcase copy, generic SaaS landing page templates, and any UI states that appear broken (zeroed metrics, dead timers, empty CTAs).

## Design Principles

1. Conversion before spectacle: every section must advance booking, registration, or inquiry.
2. Proof over promise: trust signals, metrics, and real-world visuals must back every claim.
3. Premium industrial clarity: luxury tone without abstract or poetic copy.
4. B2B utility first: exhibitors and buyers should find key actions instantly.
5. Bilingual clarity: local business language should be clear, readable, and respectful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA with strong contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion respect, and mobile readability under bright outdoor conditions.
